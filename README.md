# Brazilian Real Estate Market Analysis

Analysis of homes for sale in Brazil to explore regional price differences
and the relationship between home size and price in southern Brazil.
Completed as part of the WorldQuant University Applied Data Science Lab.

## Questions
1. How do home prices differ across Brazilian regions?
2. How strongly are home size and price related in the South?

## Methods
- Cleaned and merged two datasets (dropped nulls, split lat/lon, extracted state,
  converted BRL to USD at 3.19)
- Explored distributions with histograms and box plots
- Compared mean price by region (bar chart)
- Analyzed price vs. area in Rio Grande do Sul (scatter plot) and computed
  correlations by state
